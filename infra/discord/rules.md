# Welcome to the Des Moines tech community

This server is for anyone who works in, studies, or is curious about tech in central Iowa, and for the user groups that meet here.

**1. Be kind.** Treat people the way you would at a meetup. No harassment, insults or discrimination.

**2. Use the right channel.** Anything about tech in Des Moines goes in <#${general}>: code, product, data, design, business, careers. Everything else goes in <#${off_topic}>. Each user group has a chat channel in its own section, for that group's meetups and topics.

**3. Jobs go in <#${jobs}>.** No job posts or recruiting anywhere else, and no cold DMs about roles.

**4. No spam.** Share your own work, talks and projects in <#${show_and_tell}>, not across every channel.

**5. Keep it safe for work.**

**Need help, or see a problem?** Message an admin or any group organizer, or use Report Message.

**Want to follow another user group, or stop following one?** Click **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list.

**Looking for meetups?** Every group's events are at <https://dsmtechevents.com>. RSVP on the group's own page.
