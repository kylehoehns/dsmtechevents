# Welcome to the Des Moines tech community

This server is for anyone who works in, studies, or is curious about tech in central Iowa, and for the user groups that meet here.

1. **Be kind.** Treat people the way you would at a meetup. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
2. **Use the right channel.** Anything about tech in Des Moines goes in <#${general}>: code, testing, product, data, design, ops, security, careers. Everything else goes in <#${off_topic}>. Each user group has its own chat channel for its meetups and topics.
3. **Jobs go in <#${jobs}>.** No job posts or recruiting anywhere else, and no cold DMs about roles.
4. **No spam.** Share your own work, talks and projects in <#${show_and_tell}>, not across every channel.
5. **Keep it safe for work.**
6. **Make it readable.** Paste code and errors as text, not screenshots, and describe your images.

**Need help, or see a problem?** Message an admin privately: they're at the top of the member list (on a phone, swipe left). If it's urgent, ping <@&${admin}>.

**Want to follow another user group, or stop following one?** Open **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list (on a phone, swipe right first).

**Don't see your group here?** Say so in <#${general}>.

**Looking for meetups?** Every one has a post in <#${events}>, and they're all at <https://dsmtechevents.com>.
