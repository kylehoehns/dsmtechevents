# Welcome, organizers

Ask anything here.

**This channel** is for organizers of every group. Your group also has its own private room, like #cijug-organizers, that only your group's organizers and the server owner can see. Admins can't. An admin could give themselves your organizer role, but they'd then show up in the room's member list.

**In your group's section** you can post in its announcements channel, pin and delete messages, manage threads, and add channels of your own (a voice room for a hack night, a channel for a study group). Start their names with your group's, like `cijug-study-group`, so they make sense in search and notifications. You can rename or delete the channels you add, but not the three every group has.

**For each meetup:** post once in your announcements channel when it's on Meetup, with the link, and ping your group's member role (for example @Pyowa member). Then press **Publish**, so servers that follow your channel get it too.

**Ping only your own members,** never @everyone or @here.

**Turn on two-factor authentication** (User Settings > My Account). The server requires it for moderating: without it, deleting and pinning do nothing.

**Please don't delete your group's category.** Its channels would fall out of it, and an admin has to rebuild it.

**Adding a co-organizer, or stepping down?** Tell an admin here, and they'll move the role.
