# Welcome, organizers

Ask anything here.

**This channel** is for organizers of every group, and admins.

**In your group's section** you can post in its announcements channel, pin and delete messages, manage threads, and add channels of your own (a voice room for a hack night, a channel for a study group). Start their names with your group's, like `cijug-study-group`, so they make sense in search and notifications. You can rename or delete the channels you add, but not the two every group has.

**For each meetup:** it gets a post in #events by itself, within a few hours of going up on Meetup, and the post stays up to date. To tell your members, post in your announcements channel and ping your group's member role (for example @Pyowa member). Then press **Publish**, so servers that follow your channel get it too.

**Ping only your own members,** never @everyone or @here.

**Turn on two-factor authentication** (User Settings > My Account). The server requires it for moderating: without it, deleting and pinning do nothing.

**Please don't delete your group's category.** Its channels would fall out of it, and an admin has to rebuild it.

**Adding a co-organizer, or stepping down?** Tell an admin here, and they'll move the role.
